Here is your daily news briefing for Tuesday, 10 November.

Tentative US–Iran deal announced; Hegseth impeachment vote looms; Abu Dhabi Ukraine talks begin.

1. World Politics.

US and Iran announced a tentative peace deal (Pakistan's PM announced it); Iran's deputy FM says operations would halt on all fronts, including Lebanon. Israel's Beirut strike hours earlier drew Iranian vows of a response.

US-mediated Russia–Ukraine talks run 10 to 11 November in Abu Dhabi, with European representatives attending.

2. US Politics.

Democrats won big in the 3 November midterms; Trump said the US "took a giant step back in history" and has questioned the results' honesty without evidence.

Post-midterm White House shake-up continues; The Hill dubs it the start of a "Stephen Miller era".

Trump told reporters he believes the Iran war is militarily "effectively over".

3. Congress.

Lame-duck session opens. Rep. Thomas Massie (R-KY) filed eight articles of impeachment against Defense Secretary Hegseth over the Iran war, forcing a House vote within two legislative days; Jeffries is a "hard yes", Trump says he'll stand by Hegseth. Senate conviction is very unlikely.

Senate invoked cloture 73 to 25 on the permitting bill (Bipartisan American Affordability and Jobs Act). Government funding expires 11 December.

4. US Foreign Policy.

Washington brokered the tentative Iran deal via Pakistan; Pakistan accuses Israel of threatening the effort.

US is discussing Ukraine security guarantees modeled on NATO Articles 4/5 and reviving the Russia–NATO Council, with snapback sanctions if Moscow violates a deal.

5. Middle East Politics.

Tentative US–Iran deal is fragile: Israel's defense minister says the Iran mission is "not complete" and Netanyahu signals "heavy blows" against Iran's proxies.

Iraq and Iran are jointly investigating an attack on Saudi Arabia reportedly launched from Iraqi territory near the Iranian border.

6. Middle East Local Politics.

Lebanon: Health Ministry says Israeli attacks since 2 March killed 4,390 and wounded 12,429 (as of 5 November); UNIFIL's mandate ends 31 December, leaving open who secures the south.

Gaza: storm flooded tents sheltering displaced people; a landslide in Deir al-Balah killed a woman and four children. West Bank raids continue (Palestinian reporting).

7. Middle East Defense News.

Israeli airstrike on Beirut's Dahiyeh killed three.

US carrier aircraft recently struck Iranian air-defence launchers on Qeshm Island; Iranian sources claim SAMs were fired at a US F/A-18 over the Strait (unverified).

Iran reportedly rebuilding parts of its ballistic missile program; concern over Houthi advances.

8. Dutch Local Headlines.

PM Rob Jetten defends skipping the 200,000-strong "Red Line" Gaza protest in Amsterdam, saying it was "not his place".

Cabinet approved its AI vision paper.

Cabinet's 20 billion euros dairy-limiting nitrogen policy (proposed 26 June) back in focus.

9. European Union News.

EU negotiating a 22nd Russia sanctions package; Kallas says Russia is "pretending" to negotiate and hopes Republican midterm losses shift US policy.

Council extended the EUMAM Ukraine training mission mandate by two years.

2028 to 34 budget (MFF) talks ongoing.

10. NATO News.

Rutte expects the war to end in a negotiated compromise; neither side likely to win outright.

Russia warned NATO over Kaliningrad as Abu Dhabi talks begin.

11. Elections.

Just happened: US midterms (3 November) largely decided; Senate seats in Mississippi (Hyde-Smith 49.1 to 48.8, 97% counted) and Alaska plus 19 House races uncalled. Colorado's Boebert trails Laubacher; recount under way in Latah County, Idaho.

Bulgaria: Iliana Iotova won the 1 November presidential runoff (about 58% in early counts) and pledges a reset with Russia.

Upcoming: no major non-US national vote surfaced in today's search; check an election calendar.

12. Africa In-Depth.

Sudan: RSF has run a drone campaign against fuel depots and power stations in army-held territory since 22 October, while the army says it tracked 180+ drones in two months. The army claims recapture of two North Kordofan areas (Kazgeil, Abu Qaoud) and is advancing in West Kordofan. The RSF has supply lines through South Sudan. The war is shifting toward infrastructure attrition.

A Russia–Africa Summit was flagged in Critical Threats' 5 November Africa File.

13. Asia/India.

Japan and Vietnam leaders pledged deeper cooperation amid China's regional influence.

Pakistan is mediating US–Iran talks.

Indonesia's OJK urges banks to brace for cyberattacks.

Unconfirmed: Iranian claims of SAM fire at a US Navy jet over the Strait of Hormuz. No independent confirmation found.
