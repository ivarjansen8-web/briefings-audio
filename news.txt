Here is your daily news briefing for Wednesday, 18 November.

US–Iran strikes flare as Trump, Xi and Putin meet in Shenzhen.

Top line: The US and Iran trade strikes again (Qeshm Island, Bahrain, Kuwait) on the eve of the first-ever Trump–Xi–Putin trilateral in Shenzhen. Houthis hold Bab el-Mandeb and push toward Aden. The Dutch cabinet survives its tax-plan crisis.

1. World Politics.

First-ever Trump–Xi–Putin trilateral set for Thursday 19 November in Shenzhen, on the sidelines of APEC. Beijing frames it as "safeguarding world peace". The White House says Trump will focus on nuclear arms control and the Iran war, though Trump says he'll raise Ukraine too. CNBC ·.

Xi and Putin met first in Shenzhen today. Xi pledged to resist trade protectionism and Putin attacked Western sanctions.

Ukraine war: Ukraine struck the Kerch (Crimean) Bridge twice in 24 hours; a road span collapsed. Russia says it downed 1,441 drones. Russian strikes killed at least 14 across Ukraine and hit Kyiv infrastructure. Forbes ·.

Peace diplomacy: Zelensky held online talks with Witkoff, Kushner and European partners. The Kremlin rejects a temporary ceasefire, and Moscow calls Ukrainian NATO membership "categorically unacceptable". Conflict Tracker ·.

Canada seeks a "step-by-step" trade reset with China to reduce reliance on the US, says FM Anand.

The IMF now forecasts Ukraine's economy contracting in both 2026 and 2027.

2. US Politics.

Iran "dirty bomb" intel: CNN, citing seven officials, reports Iran is developing plans to target senior administration officials in DC with a dirty bomb or chemical weapon. Officials are divided on Iran's capability and intent. Tehran denies it, and FM Araghchi called it "rubbish". CNN ·.

Trump has withdrawn Heidi Overton's FDA nomination over insufficient congressional support, sources say.

A Pakistani man was sentenced to 27+ years for an Iran-directed 2024 plot to kill Trump.

The Supreme Court rejected a Trump appeal defending cuts to diversity-related grants.

Trump must sit for a deposition by 22 January in his 10 billion dollars defamation suit against the BBC.

3. Congress.

House Democrats vote today (closed-door) on their leadership. Jeffries runs unopposed for speaker-nominee but forces a recorded vote to box in holdouts. In the Senate, Schumer faces a Van Hollen challenge for majority leader. Al Jazeera ·.

Alaska Senate: Reuters reports that US media projected a win for Democrat Mary Peltola over incumbent Dan Sullivan, which would give Democrats a 56 to 44 Senate majority. This is from Reuters, not an official tally.

Lame-duck agenda: The Senate is advancing a permitting-reform bill (the Bipartisan American Affordability and Jobs Act). Year-end funding deadlines loom, and the House is out for Thanksgiving until 1 December. A House bid to impeach Hegseth failed 211 to 204 last week. UBS ·.

The Navy secretary nominee acknowledged at his confirmation hearing that the Iran war is straining military resources.

4. US Foreign Policy.

Trump in Manila: He announced expanded defence cooperation with the Philippines and possible civil-nuclear work, and claimed a "very big trade deal" (no details, and Manila has not confirmed it). He also announced critical-minerals deals at the Quad. Reuters via Forth ·.

Trump says he may meet Kim Jong Un during the China leg.

UN Ambassador Waltz in Lebanon: He urged Hezbollah to disarm ("one state, one army") and said UNIFIL's role "must change". The US is exploring a small replacement force when UNIFIL's mandate ends at year's end. CBS ·.

Rubio called Bahrain's FM after the Iranian attack; Washington condemned the strikes.

Hegseth visits Colombia today to deliver the first of several C-130s.

5. Middle East Politics.

Iran war escalates again after a about 2-month lull. The IRGC hit a US radar in Bahrain, the US struck an IRGC missile site on Qeshm Island, and Gulf states report intercepting Iranian and Houthi missiles and drones today. Reuters via Forth ·.

Gaza: the Board of Peace's first annual report to the UNSC warns the ceasefire could collapse. It says Israel hasn't let the technocratic administration start work, which is delaying the stabilization force. "A ceasefire that delivers nothing visible cannot be sustained indefinitely."

Iran: Leaders are worried about the public mood as gas shortages compound war damage under the US blockade. Reuters via Forth ·.

Oman and Iran FMs discussed "safeguarding" shipping lanes; Hormuz remains effectively contested.

6. Middle East Local Politics.

Israel: The Eisenkot bloc took the Knesset speakership (Yashar's Matan Kahana elected 66 to 52). Yashar–Likud unity talks show no progress; Netanyahu is reportedly open to dropping Ben Gvir. Eisenkot met Czech PM Babiš, his first foreign-leader meeting. Times of Israel ·.

Iraq: PM al-Zaidi ordered troops to surround Kataib Hezbollah's Jurf al-Sakhar stronghold over oil smuggling, and KH vowed to "confront" them. Quds Force chief Qaani flew to Baghdad to mediate; Coordination Framework sources claim a deal letting some PMF/security units enter. Foreign Exchanges ·.

Iraq–Lebanon: Al-Zaidi is visiting Beirut to explore a Tripoli–Kirkuk–Baniyas oil route that bypasses Hormuz. Iraq has resumed flows on the rehabilitated Kirkuk–Ceyhan pipeline. Al Jazeera ·.

Bahrain: A court gave dozens of Shiite clerics (64 defendants in total) life sentences for colluding with Iran; Tehran condemned it. FDD ·.

West Bank: A settler attack near Jericho killed a Palestinian woman and wounded 10.

7. Middle East Defense News.

The US struck an IRGC launch site on Qeshm Island; earlier, Iran's drone attack on Bahrain was defeated "without damage" (CENTCOM). Kuwait intercepted drones overnight.

A third US aircraft carrier has arrived in the region.

Hormuz: A tanker north of Khasab was struck twice (UKMTO), and 21 Indian crew were evacuated. The UN counts 130+ vessel incidents in the strait since the war began. ABC ·.

Yemen: Saudi-backed forces withdrew from Bab el-Mandeb and Dhubab on 15 November, and the Houthis are now about 40 mi from Aden. Riyadh is reinforcing Tur al-Baha, Saudi strikes hit Sanaa, and a Houthi drone was intercepted near Riyadh. AP/KNSS ·.

Lebanon/Syria: Israel made a rare strike in the Beqaa (Janta), hitting an arms shipment bound for Hezbollah, and carried out raids and strikes in Syria's Daraa countryside. FDD ·.

Iraq: Sirens sounded at the Khor Mor gas field after a drone overflight.

12. Africa In-Depth.

Sudan: SAF has returned to West Kordofan amid growing RSF defections (ACLED analysis) and is reinforcing the Blue Nile front under new command. The RSF's drone campaign continues, and Khartoum accuses Ethiopia of hosting RSF drone sites; the two governments trade proxy accusations, and Ethiopia expelled six Sudanese diplomats earlier this month. Why it matters: the war is regionalising along the Ethiopia–Sudan border. ACLED · CFR ·.

DRC Ebola (Bundibugyo): 11,376 confirmed cases and 5,568 deaths as of 15 November; it is now the second-largest Ebola outbreak on record. Eight provinces are affected, there is spread to Kenya and Nigeria, and no strain-specific vaccine exists. Africa CDC says it is "still not under control". ECDC ·.
